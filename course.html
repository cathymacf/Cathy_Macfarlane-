Honest answers

Questions you might have

Do I need qualifications or to be a nurse?

No. We start with non-clinical support. The "university of life" — your years caring for family — qualifies you more than you think. You'll add the right safety training and checks along the way.

How much can I really earn part-time?

Here are my own real numbers: about $45,000 in care-client revenue over 6 months, part-time (~23 hrs/week), which is roughly $75/hour and extrapolates to ~$90,000 a year. That's a gross figure — you'll cover your own costs — but it's real and honest, not hype. The $100k is a target, not a promise. See your accountant.

Is this only for people in Tasmania?

We're launching Tasmania-first so I can be a real, local guide. The model itself works anywhere — but starting close to home means I can actually help you in person. And honestly — if you're not in Tasmania, don't let that stop you. Get in touch and we'll see how to get the ball rolling; I'd genuinely love to help wherever you are.

What if it's not for me?

The first modules help you honestly decide whether this work fits your body, your life and your values — before you invest in more. And if the first module isn't worth it, you're not charged.
