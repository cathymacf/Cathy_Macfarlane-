Honest answers

Questions you might have

Do I need qualifications or to be a nurse?

No. We start with non-clinical support — the everyday help people need to stay at home. The "university of life" — the years you've spent caring for family — qualifies you more than you think. You'll add the right safety training and checks along the way.

How much can I really earn part-time?

Honestly, the same hour you'd be paid roughly $35–40 as an employee can bill around $58–73 as your own business. That's a gross figure — you'll cover your own costs — but a good, honest part-time setup can realistically target $100k a year without burning out. We teach the numbers, not the hype.

Is this only for people outside the cities?

The course is built rural-first — because regional Australia is where the need is biggest and good help is hardest to find. But the model works anywhere; the door's open to everyone.

What if it's not for me?

The first modules help you honestly decide whether this work fits your body, your life and your values — before you invest in more. That honesty is part of the course.

Why a founding waitlist?

So you can prove the course is worth it before you pay full price, and so I can build it around what you actually need. Founders get the best price and a real say.
